WHO IS PROTECTED FROM WHOM

Two parties, one shared proof

Insurer

Owns the secret model, and cannot renege on it.

Pricing thresholds and loading factors are the insurer's intellectual property, and publishing them invites adverse selection. They are encrypted to the enclave and never appear on-chain, in a log, or in an error message.

Farmer

Gets an automatic payout, with no blind trust.

The trigger data is attested by Flare's Data Connector and the decision carries a TEE signature that the settlement contract verifies. Nobody has to take the insurer at their word, and nobody has to file a claim.

Try it yourself

Both actions below are permissionless on the deployed contracts, so you can run them from your own account without anyone granting you anything. You will request a real FDC attestation, wait for a Flare voting round, and settle whatever the sealed model decides.

Already settled

A policy pays at most once and then closes, so each entry above is single use. The wait for a voting round is 90 to 180 seconds and cannot be shortened, so the log tells you which round it is waiting on rather than showing a spinner. Settling needs the enclave's signed result, which is served by the read-only bridge in scripts/result-bridge.sh.

The dashboard is read-only and needs no wallet. The "Try it yourself" section is the only part that sends a transaction, and it signs everything through your own wallet. This page never asks for, stores, or transmits a private key.

Contract state comes from the Coston2 RPC endpoint, event history from the Coston2 explorer API, and enclave state from the extension's own GET /state endpoint.
